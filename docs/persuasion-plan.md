The learner approved the free checklist as the second application; it is implemented in both storefronts. No conversion outcome has been measured.

## One actual element, explained

The existing size selector updates the displayed fictional price (S $26, M $28, L $30, XL $32 USD); bag rows retain their unit price. This helps visitors see the cost before adding an item and avoids hiding a size-related price change. It is useful transparency, not counted as a Cialdini application or a voluntary commitment.

## Guardrails and presentation

Use identical checklist and invitation copy in both styles. Modernist: aligned rules and clear hierarchy. Postmodernist: framed editorial panels, with level, legible body text. Do not invent reviews, users, scarcity, credentials, fabric composition, durability tests or proven mountain suitability. Do not imply that this T-shirt is sufficient equipment for a mountain climb. No conversion improvement is claimed. Required size selection alone does not establish consistency.
