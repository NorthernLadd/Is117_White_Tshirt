    WHY THIS DIRECTION

    02 / Myth meets mountain

    Reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype, 1981, made by Fausto Celati for Memphis, V&A, M.1-2011. Visually inspected: a blue semicircular body, small wheels, and bright bulb sockets turn a lamp into a toy-like hybrid.

    • A circular frame and poster stamp layer different visual languages around the unchanged shirt.
    • Classical serif emphasis quotes the mythic Olympus name against functional sans-serif labels.
    • Offset panels and a diagonal ridge create theatrical tension while body text and controls remain level and legible.

    Intended effect: make the climb feel like a personal heroic story; retain a straightforward offer for the casual hiker.

    Risk: theatrical layering may distract from the product or feel less practical. Product information remains in an orderly panel below.
