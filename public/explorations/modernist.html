    WHY THIS DIRECTION

    01 / Structured ascent

    Reference: Edward McKnight Kauffer, Drawing, 1934, V&A, E.3770-2004. Visually inspected: jointed black geometric parts form a figure across a tilted pale plane.

    • A repeatable grid aligns navigation, message, product, and size controls: function leads the composition.
    • Heavy sans-serif hierarchy and a diagonal ridge turn the upward idea into a clear reading path.
    • Flat shapes and system fonts keep production simple and repeatable, rather than reproducing the drawing.

    Intended effect: ordered, deliberate confidence for the mountain athlete; easy price and size comparison for the day hiker.

    Risk: the technical visual language may imply performance we have not substantiated. Explicit demo wording counters that inference.
