06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Hero character expressed through direct, encouraging language. The proposed voice traits remain open for learner confirmation.

Proposed voice / bold, determined, encouraging

“For the climb you choose. For the resolve you bring.”

Lead with resolve. Keep the practical next step clear: choose your size.

Claims stay grounded

Durability is a product goal, not a verified fact. Do not claim this shirt is proven mountain gear, exclusive equipment, or a guarantee of reaching a summit. Prices are fictional demo data. Two ethical persuasion applications remain to be agreed.

07 / Persuasion plan

Useful.
Voluntary. Honest.

AI proposals for both styles. Not active on the storefront yet; learner choice and visitor-benefit explanation are pending.

Reciprocity / Give useful help first

Before buying: evaluate any shirt.

Proposed free checklist beside the offer: inspect the shirt you already own for wear; check its actual care label; ask for verified fabric, fit and durability information before deciding.

No purchase, email or enrollment required. Helps the mountain athlete evaluate repeated-use needs without treating our demo as verified equipment.

Unity / A shared value, openly offered

Your summit. Your pace.

Proposed identity-panel copy: Olympus is for the resolve you bring, whether your next goal is a summit or a day hike.

An invitation to identify with perseverance, not a claim that an existing club or customer group endorses the product. Welcomes the secondary hiker without demanding an extreme achievement.

Sources: Cialdini: reciprocity; Cialdini on unity, ASU interview. These are intended mechanisms, not tested conversion results. No reviews, scarcity, credentials or product-performance facts are supplied.
