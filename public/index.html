A FREE DECISION TOOL

Before buying:
evaluate any shirt.

Use this checklist with a shirt you already own. No signup or purchase needed.

  1. Look for wear. Inspect the seams and fabric of your current shirt and note anything that needs attention.
  2. Read its care label. Check the instructions supplied with that actual shirt rather than guessing.
  3. Ask for evidence. Before choosing another shirt, ask for verified fabric, fit and durability information relevant to your intended use.

Consider whether what you already own meets your needs. Olympus demo fabric, fit and durability information has not been verified.
